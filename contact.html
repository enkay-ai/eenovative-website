FAQs

Common questions

How quickly can a project start?

Most engagements begin with a discovery call within a few days of your enquiry. Automation sprints and audits typically start within one to two weeks.

Do you publish pricing?

No — prices are prepared individually after a discovery conversation. This keeps the offer fair, flexible and aligned to each client's exact requirements, scope and timeline.

What happens to my enquiry data?

Form submissions are stored securely in our lead management system, used only to respond to your enquiry, and never shared with third parties.

Do you work outside education?

Yes. Alongside schools and trusts we support SMEs, startups, hospitality teams, community organisations and public interest projects.

Can programmes be delivered at our venue?

Yes — digital literacy sessions and AI & Coding Club programmes are designed for delivery in schools, workplaces and community venues, with resources adapted to your setting.
