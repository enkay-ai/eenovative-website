About

Bridging education
and technology

Eenovative Technologies was built from the journey of a Bengaluru-born, London-based technologist, educator and data scientist — focused on practical AI, digital literacy and technology adoption that actually lands.

Mission

To bridge education and technology by making AI, automation and digital systems understandable, accessible and useful.

  • Practical, measurable outcomes
  • Human-centred and safe by design
  • Education-first, community impact

Values

Practical impact, trust, safety, inclusion, sustainability, measurable ROI and continuous learning — in that order, every project.

Positioning

A technology consultancy for organisations that want simple, secure and scalable AI-enabled systems without unnecessary complexity.

Trust, safety & sustainability

Responsible from day one

Technology should be useful, inclusive and responsible — these principles are built into every delivery, not bolted on.

Responsible AI

Clear use cases, human review, data minimisation and transparent limitations for every AI-assisted system we build.

Security first

MFA, access control, role-based permissions, backup thinking and practical staff guidance as standard.

Inclusive design

Plain language, accessible resources, flexible learning routes and support for varied confidence levels.

Why "Eenovative"

Education-first innovation

The name carries the founding idea: innovation that begins in education. Our approach was shaped by years of building real systems inside a school — reporting platforms, operations dashboards, automation workflows — and teaching the people who use them. That combination of building and teaching is what makes our technology stick.
